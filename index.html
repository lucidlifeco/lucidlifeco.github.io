Fair questions

Before you reach out

What does it cost?

The Briefing and the Profit Sit-Down are free. The Operator Review is a flat fee, credited toward any work we do together after it. Ongoing work is priced by scope, and you'll know the number before you agree to anything.

How much of my time does it take?

The Briefing takes about 30 minutes a day for a week. The Sit-Down is 45 minutes. After that, most owners spend an hour or two a week putting fixes in place.

Do I have to share my numbers?

Yes, at the Sit-Down and after. Your sales, your quotes and what's owed to you are where the leaks show up. They stay between us.

What if it doesn't work for my business?

Then I'll tell you at the Sit-Down, before you spend anything. I'd rather say no than take money for work that won't pay you back.

Do you do marketing, ads or websites?

Not first. Marketing comes after the leaks are fixed. When you're ready, I set the strategy and bring in trusted partners for the content and the website.
