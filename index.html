The Profit Chain

Every business makes money through the same five links.

A plumber, a dental practice, a bookkeeper, a boutique. Different work, same chain. A weak link leaks profit no matter how hard you push on the others.

And the links multiply. Small gains in each one compound into a much bigger result than a big push in one.
